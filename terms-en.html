By using Ped In Picture ("app"), you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

2. What the app is (and what it is not)

Ped In Picture is an educational tool, made to help healthcare professionals explain pediatric surgical conditions to patients and families, using illustrations and real photos.

The app does not provide diagnoses, does not give medical advice and does not replace an in-person medical evaluation. The information shown explains a condition already evaluated by a professional — it must not be used to self-diagnose or to decide on a treatment on your own.

3. Who can use it

The app is aimed at healthcare professionals and, through them, at patients and families during a consultation or at home, when the professional shares a script. You must have the legal capacity to accept these terms.

4. Account and subscriptions

Today the app has a free tier and a paid tier that are simulated — no real charge happens yet. When real payment is turned on:

  • Professional plan: unlocks creating and sharing Storyboard scripts and the rest of the library.
  • Family plan (for parents and guardians): unlocks opening, without the free limit, the scripts that a professional shared with you.
  • Billing is handled by the App Store (Apple) or the Play Store (Google). Cancellation and refunds follow those stores' rules, not ours.
  • Sign-in (when turned on) is optional for those who only use their phone and serves to use the same subscription on more than one device.

5. Sharing a script with a family

If you are a professional and you share a script:

  • The app generates an invite code. Without a Family plan subscription, the family can open the script only 1 time (re-opening within 1 hour after the first opening does not count). After that, they need to subscribe to the Family plan. Tell the family before sending.
  • The invite is valid for 30 days and you can cancel it whenever you want.
  • You are responsible for having the family's awareness before sharing and for what you write in the notes. Do not write data that identifies the child or health details in the notes. The invite only carries the child's first name if you tick that option; by default it goes without a name.
  • Only share with the child's guardian.

If you are a parent or guardian and you open a shared script: the content is an educational explanation prepared by the professional who looks after the child. It does not replace medical advice — if in doubt, talk to the professional.
