Privacy Policy — Ped In Picture (PiP)

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Who we are

Ped In Picture ("app", "we") is developed and maintained by Eduardo Botelho Fontes. For any privacy question, contact: cipeparatodos@gmail.com.

2. What the app is for

Ped In Picture is an educational tool that helps healthcare professionals explain pediatric surgical conditions to patients and families, using illustrations and photos. The app does not provide diagnoses and does not replace an in-person medical evaluation.

3. What data we collect

a) Data kept only on your own device (not sent to us):

  • The language you chose.
  • Collections you build in the Scrapbook (which library photos you saved).
  • Scripts you build in the Storyboard, including the name you give the character (a made-up name; the app asks you not to use the child's real name) and the notes you write on each scene. This only leaves your device if you use the share function (see item c).
  • Photos and scenes downloaded for use without internet.
  • Whether you have already seen the app's first notice.

b) If you turn on sign-in (an optional feature, for professionals): We collect your email address, used only to send you a sign-in link and keep your session connected, in case you want to use your subscription on more than one device. We do not ask for a password. We also keep which plan you have and until when it is valid.

c) If a professional shares a Storyboard with parents: The app creates an invite (a code) and stores on our servers only:

  • the journey, the character and the list of scenes of the script;
  • the character's name (a made-up name; the app asks the professional not to use the child's real name), only if the professional ticks that option when sharing (by default the invite goes without a name; the app and the server cut everything after the first word);
  • the professional's notes, only if the professional ticks that option when sharing. For this reason, the professional must not write data that identifies the child or health details in the notes; the app warns about this.

The invite is linked to the anonymous identifier of the professional's device (the same one described in item e below; it is not an e-mail or an account), and that is how the professional can cancel it. The invite is valid for 30 days and the professional can cancel it at any time. Expired or cancelled invites can no longer be opened and are deleted from our servers within 30 days afterwards. We never store photos of the child, surname, date of birth or documents.

d) When parents open an invite: The app creates, without the parents having to do anything, an anonymous identifier (a random string of letters and numbers, with no name, email or phone number). We store this identifier, which invites it opened and how many times — this is how we apply the limit of free openings. If the parents subscribe to the Family plan, the subscription is linked to that same identifier.

e) When you open the app (everyone): the app silently creates an anonymous identifier for this installation (a random string of letters and numbers, with no name, email or phone). When the app asks for an image from the collection, our server records, per day, which images were released for that identifier (or, if there is none, for the IP address), only to limit mass downloading of the collection. This log is deleted within a few days and contains no name, email, storyboards or collections. We also keep, with no identifier at all, only the daily total per image (how many devices viewed it), to learn which topics are most useful and where to create new images. This total does not reveal who viewed what.

f) Anonymous usage statistics: to learn which features are used (Canvas, Storyboard, Scrapbook and the plans screen) and to size our servers, the app adds up on the device how many times each one was used and how many images were shown from the device's memory or had to be downloaded, and sends only the totals, at most once a day. Only the plan (free or subscriber), the language and the system (Android or iPhone) go along. We do not send any identifier, images, names, notes or which content you viewed, and we cannot tell who did what. You can turn this off at any time on the Account screen.

g) Anonymous crash reports: if the app crashes or an error happens, it may send a technical report to Sentry (our crash-reporting service), only if "Anonymous usage statistics" is turned on in the Account screen (it is on by default; you can turn it off at any time and sending stops immediately). The report contains: the type of error, the place in the code where it happened, the names of the screens you went through, the app version, the phone model and the Android or iOS version. It does not contain your name, e-mail, IP address, device name, images, storyboards, children's names or internet addresses with access keys. We use the reports only to fix errors.

h) What we do NOT collect: we do not access your camera, photo gallery, contacts or location, and we do not use third-party tracking, advertising or behavior-analytics tools. The only statistics are the anonymous totals described in item (f) and the crash reports described in item (g).

i) Payments: subscription payments are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We never see or store your card details.

4. Who we share data with

  • Supabase (hosting/infrastructure) — stores the email of those who turn on sign-in, the sharing invites and the anonymous identifiers, and keeps a backup copy of the library images.
  • Cloudflare (image hosting) — delivers the library and journey images to the app. When the app downloads an image, Cloudflare receives that request (like any internet server, this includes the device's IP address). It does not receive your name, email, invites or scripts.
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) — receives the identifier (anonymous or account) and the information that a subscription exists and until when it is valid, in order to unlock the plans. Only when payment is active in the app.
  • Sentry (crash reports) — receives the anonymous technical reports described in item (g) of section 3, only when "Anonymous usage statistics" is turned on.
  • Apple / Google — to process the payment, when you subscribe.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers.

Some of these providers may store data on servers outside Brazil. We require protection from them that is compatible with the LGPD (Brazil's General Data Protection Law).

5. Your rights

Under the LGPD, you may at any time:

  • Ask to see what data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct wrong data.
  • Ask us to delete the data linked to your device, an invite you created, or an invite that contains a child's name.

For any of these requests, follow the steps below. Because the device identifier is anonymous, we may need your help to locate it (for example, by telling us the code of an invite you created or opened).

How to request deletion of your data

These are the steps to request deletion of your data in the Ped In Picture app:

  • Step 1. Write to cipeparatodos@gmail.com with the subject "Delete data - Ped In Picture".
  • Step 2. If the request is about an invite, include its 8-letter code. Whoever created an invite can also cancel it inside the app, on the share screen.
  • Step 3. We reply and complete the request within 15 days.

What we delete: the invites you created (journey, character, scenes and, if you ticked them, the character name and notes); the record of which invites your device opened; the Familia access linked to your identifier; and the subscription record kept on our server when there is no active subscription.

What we keep: anonymous daily totals of use and of images (no identifier; they cannot tell who did what); the technical daily log of released images, which deletes itself within a few days; and the purchase data held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat, which follow their own policies and which we cannot delete.

Storage periods: expired or cancelled invites are deleted automatically within 30 days after they expire or are cancelled. All other data is deleted when the request is completed.

6. Children

The app is intended for healthcare professionals and the guardians of children, not for children directly. No data is collected directly from a child. The only data about a child that may reach our servers is the character's name (made up, not the child's) and the notes the professional wrote, but only if the professional ticks each of those options, and only when the professional decides to share the Storyboard with the guardian.

7. Security

We use standard security practices (encrypted connection, access control on the server, a limit on attempts against anyone trying to guess invite codes) to protect the data. The app cannot read other people's invites: all access goes through rules checked on the server. No system is 100% infallible, but we take this seriously.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects or how we use data, we update this page and the date at the top.

9. Contact
